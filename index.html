What is TechBridge?

TechBridge is an educational initiative by Baselink Services Limited. We bridge the gap between theoretical learning and real-world application by providing practical, project-based programs in high-demand tech fields. Our goal is to prepare participants for actual workplace challenges, not just exams.

Our Programs

Two career-focused programs designed to build real skills.

Data Analytics

Learn to collect, clean, analyze, and visualize data. Master tools like Excel, SQL, Python, and modern dashboards to turn raw data into actionable insights.

Web Development

Build modern, responsive websites and web applications. From HTML and CSS to JavaScript and frameworks, develop the skills employers actually look for.

30-Day Practical Internship

Put your skills to work. Our internship gives you hands-on experience in a real project environment, not just theory.

  • Work on real, project-based assignments
  • Receive a recommendation letter based on performance
  • Apply classroom knowledge to practical challenges
  • Build a portfolio of completed work
